Polygon GA · decomposition

Splits a polygon into parts with the areas you ask for, using straight cuts. Every cut is placed so the areas are exact; a genetic algorithm chooses the cut directions that give the best-shaped parts.

Edit the polygon by dragging its points or edges; CTRL + click adds a point. Keyboard: 0 runs, Esc stops.

Developed by Mehmet Akif AKKUS using paper.js, nerdamer.js and numeric.js.
