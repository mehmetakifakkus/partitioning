Polygon Decomposition with a Genetic Algorithm

About

This web application splits a simple polygon into sub-polygons whose areas follow the given parcel distribution. Every cut is placed so that the areas are exact; a genetic algorithm chooses the cut directions that give the best-shaped parts.

Modify the polygon: drag the points or line segments, or hold CTRL and click to add a new point. Keyboard: 0 runs the algorithm.
